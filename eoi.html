Expression of Interest (EOI) for Strategic University Projects

SUDGEC invites qualified contractors for architectural, structural, solar, water, road, and building works at the University of Education and Entrepreneurship, Akamkpa, Cross River State, Nigeria.

Register Online View Requirements

Background

The Sustainable Development Goals Evaluation Programme (SUDGEC), aligned with the UN SDGs initiative, invites qualified and reputable contractors and consultants to provide architectural, structural, and construction services for strategic infrastructure projects at the University of Education and Entrepreneurship, Akamkpa.

This intervention supports sustainable infrastructure development, capacity building, and inclusive growth in line with national development priorities and global best practices.

Client & Project Beneficiary

SUDGEC PROJECT
Corporate Partner & Project Beneficiary: University of Education and Entrepreneurship, Akamkpa, Cross River State, Nigeria.

Description of Projects & Scope

Modern University Guest House

  • 15-Room Guest House
  • 10 Self-Contained Staff Quarters

Entrepreneurial Development Centre

Capacity for 350 trainees to promote skills, entrepreneurship, and sustainable development.

NUC-Standard Student Lecture Halls

  • 5 Units of lecture halls built to NUC standards

Student Hostels

  • 500-Bed Male Hostel
  • 500-Bed Female Hostel
  • Rehabilitation of dilapidated hostel blocks

Road Infrastructure

Construction of 10 kilometers of roads at various locations within the university campus.

Renewable Energy & Water Infrastructure

  • Supply and installation of solar street lights
  • Construction of solar-powered boreholes with overhead water tanks

Mandatory Submission Requirements

Interested companies are required to submit hard copies of their EOI documents with the following:

Corporate & Legal Documents

  • CAC Certificate of Incorporation
  • Certified Memorandum & Articles of Association
  • Company profile with organizational structure
  • Evidence of registration on sustainsudgecorg.org

Statutory & Regulatory Compliance

  • Tax Clearance Certificate (last 3 years)
  • Pension Compliance Certificate (PENCOM)
  • Industrial Training Fund (ITF) Compliance Certificate
  • NSITF Compliance Certificate

Technical & Financial Capacity

  • Audited financial statements or evidence of financial capability
  • List & CVs of key technical personnel
  • Evidence of similar projects executed
  • List of equipment and plant

Sworn Affidavits & EOI Payment

  • Sworn affidavit of authenticity and non-blacklisting
  • Evidence of EOI registration payment (receipt, teller, reference)

Mode of Submission & General Conditions

Submit in sealed envelopes clearly marked: "Expression of Interest (EOI) Document for Architectural, Structural Design, Supply, Consultancy, Supervision, Solar Street Light Installation, Borehole Project, Road Construction and Building Works at the University of Education and Entrepreneurship, Akamkpa".

  • Submission does not constitute commitment to award a contract.
  • Only companies completing online registration & EOI-PAYMENT considered.
  • Shortlisted companies invited to next stage of procurement.
  • The client reserves the right to accept/reject any EOI without reason.
  • All processes follow Public Procurement Act, 2007.

Conclusion

This Expression of Interest represents an opportunity for competent contractors to participate in a strategic development initiative aimed at strengthening infrastructure, entrepreneurship, and sustainable development within the University of Education and Entrepreneurship, Akamkpa.
